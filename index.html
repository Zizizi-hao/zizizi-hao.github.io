Background & directions

A little about me

I am a third-year Ph.D. student at Southeast University and the Shenzhen Loop Area Institute (SLAI). My advisor at Southeast University is Prof. Ziyuan Pu. At SLAI, I worked with Prof. Hongyang Li in my first year and am now supervised by Prof. Ping Luo.

I received my M.S. in Transportation from Hunan University in 2024, advised by Prof. Chenhui Liu. My earlier research on driving behavior and traffic safety informs my current work on autonomous systems.

Looking ahead

I am interested in 3D and 4D world-action models, reinforcement learning and post-training for safety, and adapting agent harnesses to physical tasks such as driving and manipulation.
