I am a third-year Ph.D. student at Southeast University and Shenzhen Loop Area Institution (SLAI). My tutor in Southeast University is Prof. Ziyuan Pu. In SLAI first year, I am supervised by Prof. Hongyang Li. And now I am supervised by Prof. Ping Luo.

Prior to joining Southeast University, I earned my M.S. (2024) in Transportation from Hunan University, supervised by Prof. Chenhui Liu.

My research focuses on autonomous driving and Embodied AI, particularly End-to-End Autonomous Driving, 3D object detection, trajectory prediction, and driving behavior analysis.

  • Driving Behavior Analysis: Elderly drivers, delivery riders, and vulnerable road users
  • Roadside Perception: Infrastructure-based sensing systems
